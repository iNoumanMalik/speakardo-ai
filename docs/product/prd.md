# **Speakardo Product Requirements Document (PRD)** 

Version: 1.0 Status: Founder Draft Product: Speakardo Category: AI Life Assistant Author: Nouman Malik 

# **1. Executive Summary** 

Speakardo is an AI-powered life assistant designed to help users remember, organize, plan, and eventually automate parts of their daily lives through natural conversation. 

Unlike traditional reminder apps that require users to fill forms, configure dates, and manage lists manually, Speakardo allows users to simply communicate their intentions naturally. 

Example: 

"Remind me to call mom after work." 

Speakardo understands the request, extracts relevant information, schedules the reminder, and later delivers it through the user's preferred notification channel. 

The long-term vision is to evolve Speakardo from a reminder application into a persistent AI companion that remembers context, predicts needs, and autonomously assists users in managing their personal and professional lives. 

# **2. Product Vision** 

To become the world's most trusted AI life assistant that helps people remember, organize, and execute everything that matters. 

# **3. Mission Statement** 

Help people spend less mental energy remembering things and more energy living their lives. 

# **4. Problem Statement** 

Modern life creates an overwhelming number of tasks, commitments, appointments, responsibilities, and personal goals. 

1 

People frequently forget: 

- Medicines 

- Meetings 

- Birthdays 

- Bills 

- Daily routines 

- Follow-ups 

- Personal commitments 

Current reminder applications have several limitations: 

- Require manual form filling 

- Require selecting dates and times 

- Require navigating multiple screens 

- Do not understand natural language 

- Do not learn from user behavior 

- Do not proactively help users 

As a result, users either avoid creating reminders or abandon reminder applications entirely.
